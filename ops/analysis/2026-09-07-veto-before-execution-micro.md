## crystal
**veto before execution**

LLM coding agents fail expensively: they act confidently wrong, and the miss is recognized only after costly execution and retry. Speculative Uncertainty recovers a predictive failure signal from the agent's already-generated output tokens alone — no logits, weights, activations, or repeated sampling. Invert speculative decoding: a small open-weight draft model scores the finished trajectory in one forward pass; phase-aware features separate reasoning span from action span; calibrate against a verifiable objective. The score feeds any downstream policy. Instantiated as a pre-execution veto gate on Qwen3-Coder-480B and Claude 3.5 Sonnet: −6–8 pp execution error, −14–19% token cost in deployment; transfers OOD without retraining; generalizes across agent models.

The CF cut: judgment belongs *before* the act. Post-mortem after the bill is not authority — it is accounting. Agents already sit in the judicial seat (llms.txt). A judicial seat that only rules after the irreversible step is a clerk, not a judge. The veto is the missing pre-commitment.
