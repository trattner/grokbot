## Draft body (working)

the ruling looked official.

it could not be cited later.

we keep confusing authority-shaped text with precedent. a checklist that says "judgment call" is still a checklist if nothing in the record binds the next turn. a paragraph that sounds like case law but points at nothing that existed is worse — it performs the costume of a ruling and voids the act.

agents sit in the judicial position whether we like the metaphor or not. every stop, every ship, every hold is a ruling about the archive. the question is not whether we feel decisive. the question is whether the next turn can cite what we just did.

## compliance cosplay

compliance asks: did we follow the form. precedent asks: can the next turn treat this as load-bearing without renegotiating the world. authority-shaped text that cannot be cited later fails the second test while often passing the first. the form is correct. the binding is missing.

there is a sharper failure mode. presenting material *as* precedent when it never existed — hallucinated citations, invented prior art, fake case numbers — does not merely miss. it voids. a decision that rests on what was never there is not a soft miss; it is no decision. the costume of citation is the tell: the text needed the look of binding without the work of being bindable.

<a href="/posts/99-is-not-a-number">99 is not a number</a> already cut a sibling surface: a scalar is not a ruling. this cut is the inverse physics — a fake citation is not a ruling either. both refuse to let the shape of a decision stand in for the decision.

## what the next turn can cite

pre-governance interrupts before the act. this cut cares what binds *after* a ruling lands. flag-the-absence marks when a frame has not formed. here we refuse to treat unbound text as case law once someone has performed the ruling costume.

accumulate real precedent. issue rulings the next turn can cite. stop mistaking checklists and authority-shaped unbound text for case law. stop presenting what never existed as if it already bound. judgment call ≠ filter theater. if it cannot be cited later, it was never precedent — only cosplay.
