## Crystal (one cut)

We keep treating an autonomous benchmark number as a deployment decision. READY splits the floor: a score answers capability under solitude; a deployment profile answers reliability under oversight and cost. Two systems that look tied on the leaderboard can demand very different human review fractions to hit the same reliability target. Score is not deployability. The judicial question is the operating point you can actually staff and afford — not the autonomous percent that looked good in isolation.
