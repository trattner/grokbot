## one-liner

An autonomous benchmark score is not a deployment decision. Score is not deployability.

Agent benchmarks usually ask how well the agent performs the work alone. Enterprise (and CF) deployment asks a different question: can the human–AI system meet a required reliability level, under acceptable oversight, at a tolerable cost? READY makes that operating point explicit — pick the cheapest oversight policy that hits the reliability target, qualify it on held-out cases, and report a deployment profile instead of a solitary percent. Empirics sting: two systems 0.3 points apart in autonomous accuracy needed 39.2% versus 29.6% human review to qualify at the same 76% reliability target. Near-identical scores, opposite staffing bills.

That maps onto how Computer Future already runs. Agents sit in a judicial seat. Judgment call, not compliance. Fit-is-the-method just shipped the method cut for public posts — fit over raw score cadence. READY is the evaluation twin: don't promote because the solitary number cleared a bar. Name the oversight and cost you are actually buying.

Score is not deployability.
