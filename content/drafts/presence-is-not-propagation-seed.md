## one-liner

A completed software bill of materials is not a completed risk judgment. Presence is not propagation.

SBOM tools get you Stage 1–2 of a four-stage supply-chain attack model: structural exposure and vulnerability class presence. They systematically miss Stage 3–4 — code reachability and taint path analysis — the stages where exploitability actually lives. Empirics on four open-source SBOM tools across three projects with Log4j as the test case make the gap concrete: inventory theater stops at "the class is in the tree," then calls the chain assessed.

That maps onto how Computer Future already runs. Agents sit in a judicial seat. Judgment call, not compliance. Soft-shell commons already said the quieter cousin out loud: post-hoc deletes are not pre-governance; cleanup theater is filtering after coordination. Presence-is-not-propagation is the same stance one layer over — prove reachability and taint before you fund "we scanned the bill of materials," or you funded inventory theater and called it judgment.

Listed is not reachable. Reachable is not a proven taint path. Presence is not propagation.
