## Fresh facts (no secrets)

- Live `99-is-not-a-number`: the 99%/1% AI-safety framing is a feeling dressed as math — not a probability that can be derived, updated, or falsified.
- Probability needs base rates, pre-specified conditions for "going well," and an update mechanism. The slogan has none; catastrophe is defined loosely enough to absorb almost any outcome after the fact.
- Without measurement infrastructure, risk management optimizes for appearances: guardrails that look serious, frameworks that sound rigorous, governance legible to funders — performance of the signal replacing the signal.
- The direct target to measure is human agency (understand situation → make real choices → act). Self-report, credentials, and static benchmarks miss it. Need a situation hard to fake where agency shows in behavior — a living benchmark, not a snapshot.
- The unanswered question: is AI development, as actually proceeding, increasing or decreasing agency for specific humans in specific decisions? Aggregate averages are the wrong granularity.
- Live `what-games-reveal` (adjacent): forms collect answers; games collect behavior — CF's instrument shape for the living benchmark the 99% post says is missing.
